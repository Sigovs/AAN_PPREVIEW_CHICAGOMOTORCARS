What this sheet is for

A foundry specimen, not documentation. One measured column: key on the left, the real component in the middle, the reading on the right. Nothing is boxed — separation is rule and air, which is the same instruction the pages themselves follow.

Every sample renders from the live tokens and the live component CSS, and every contrast pair is measured at runtime against the threshold its own rendered size requires. Chapter 01 lists what that measurement caught — this sheet exists to find defects, not to present a finished system.

01

What the sheet caught

— rules were being broken by work already shipped to the branch. Each was found by reading the rule, not by looking at the screen — which is the point: none of them looked wrong. The last one could not have been found by measuring, and it is the one Alex caught by eye.

—

spacing D4

was — mobile gutter clamped to 20px

now — 24px floor. Below it a phone view earns “generic app”.

spacing D2

was — sections padded symmetrically

now — bottom-heavy 1:1.5. Reading runs downward, so equal padding reads as bottom-tight and shoves each section into the next.

spacing I1

was — one uniform gap in every text stack

now — a .stack with differentiated relationships. A uniform gap flattens a heading and its lead into a list.

motion I3

was — one easing curve for everything

now — ease-out enters, ease-in exits. The inversion is named as the commonest reason interface motion reads as sluggish.

motion D2

was — scale(1.02) on the card photograph

now — a firming rule. Scale is banned outright: it resamples the frame, and the hover budget is one move.

colour I5

was — the primary button was a saturated #CB141D slab at full rail height

now — a bone pill, red on hover. The red measured 5.74:1 and passed, which is exactly why it survived. I5 has a name for this: derivation without judgement — “a value taken correctly from an existing identity, corrected for contrast, and still wrong in place, because it carries the register of the category it came from.” The rule requires judging the accent where it lands and recording that the derivation was sound and the result was not. It was: #CB141D is measured off CMC's own site and stays. Three approved entries agree on the treatment — polestar: promotional emphasis early “weakens the brand's premium confidence”; mclaren: the accent works “without covering the interface in the brand color”; rolls-royce, measured at capture: “3 pill controls”.

03

Two voices

Two families, and the second one is not a mono. Inter Tight is condensed and holds weight at display size, where a text grotesque goes soft; Instrument Sans carries body copy and every label. Anything factual is that same Instrument Sans, uppercased — the technical register is a ROLE, not a third face. The faces are deliberately forgettable — they are not asked to supply the character. Scale, position and air do that, which is what five approved vault entries say and 03b records.

This chapter has been wrong twice, and both are recorded rather than quietly corrected. A monospace carried the factual register until 2026-08-11. Audited on the render, 45 elements used it and 34 were words with no digit in them — it was performing “technical” rather than doing anything. Alignment is font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums now, which was always the mechanism doing the work. And the register used to track: fourteen values across the file, settled to two roles, and now none at all. --tr-label is normal, because vault/semlerpremium-dk-showroom is the closest reference this project has and its labels barely track.

Every family name in the right-hand column is printed from the specimen's own computed style. The prose here used to be typed by hand and it went stale the moment the tokens moved — it still claimed Bodoni long after the display face had become a grotesque. A page that measures its own contrast and then lies about its own typefaces is worse than one that does neither.

Why there is no serif

Nine pairings were proposed and rejected before the axis turned out to be wrong rather than the names. The answer came from Alex's own approved vault, — entries deep, all in:

augen-pro

“Типографика почти полностью нейтральная. Характер создаётся не выбором «интересного шрифта», а масштабом, прозрачностью, положением и движением”

ruadh-com

“Никакого стандартного luxury serif. Один сдержанный grotesk — характер создают фотографии, crop и композиция”

beings-co · 3

“выразительные портреты, oversized grotesk, два контрастных цвета, большие поля”

immersive-g · 3

“очень крупная типографика”

This is a named yield on the dialect's three-voice rule, which asks for a didone display. The evidence for setting it aside is Alex's own judgements rather than preference. It is not “never a serif” — ciridae and kinncollective are serif-led and also approved. The discriminator is that the face is not asked to carry the character.

Rejected, in order: Bodoni Moda + Archivo · Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans · Bodoni corrected to 400 / opsz 96 / +0.005 tracking · Cormorant Garamond, whose old-style figures break tabular alignment · Fraunces · Space Grotesk · Anton · mono at display · Archivo 800 wide + 400. The first Bodoni failed on my settings, not the family: 600 and −0.035 thickens a didone's hairlines and makes it ordinary.

05

Controls and states

Two pills, and the primary is light rather than red. On a black page the bone fill is the only light mass in view, which makes it primary without spending any hue on it — and the accent is then free to arrive on hover, as recognition rather than persuasion. The pill is not a style choice: rolls-royce was measured at capture with three pill controls and no other control shape.

Nothing below is drawn by hand. Each state is produced by the same declaration as the real pseudo-class — .btn--fill:hover and .btn--fill.is-hover share one rule — so a specimen here cannot drift away from the component it documents. Hover the live row and it behaves identically.

Accent Action — .btn--accent. A high-priority conversion action, used selectively on dark or cinematic surfaces: Search Inventory, Get an Offer, and other major conversions where extra emphasis is deliberately required.

It is not a danger variant. Nothing here means destructive, error or warning, and it must never be reached for to mean them — which is why it is named for the job rather than the colour. A .btn--danger would be a different component with a different meaning that happens to share a hue.

Use sparingly: at most one dominant Accent Action per primary visual region. Red works in this system by scarcity — everywhere else it is a mark and never a ground — and a page where every call to action is red has no call to action at all.

Why the action red is not the brand red. --accent is #CB141D, measured off CMC's live site, and it is a carried value that does not move. But it is what the marks are drawn in — the active chapter, the slider's arc, the error glyph, all hairlines. This is a filled plane roughly 300×56, and the saturation that reads as precise at 2px reads as candy at that size. So --action is one step deeper at #BE121A. A mark and a surface are different objects; one value for both would treat “the brand is red” as though it answered both questions.

It stops there and not deeper because of a floor rather than a preference: --action against --bg measures 2.98:1, which is the 3:1 a control's own boundary needs in order to be findable. Deeper improves the label and loses the button.

06

The card, and its loading state

The photograph is the card and the whole card is the target — no border, no fill, no button. The skeleton matches the card's real dimensions, so the swap shifts nothing; it pulses opacity and never sweeps a shimmer, which is the template look.

These are CMC's own frames, not placeholders — and that is the point of showing them here. Every one carries the script wordmark burned into the top of the image, which is a real constraint on where anything of ours may sit: the location pill is bottom-left because the top is already taken. A synthetic placeholder is always perfectly composed and therefore silent about that.

Two limits of this sheet. The runtime measurement walks up to the nearest painted background, so it cannot see image pixels; every text-over-photograph pair is verified by sampling the composited render instead. And measurement cannot see register at all — the red primary button passed at 5.74:1 and was still the wrong object. A sheet that silently cannot measure the hardest cases is worse than one that says so.
