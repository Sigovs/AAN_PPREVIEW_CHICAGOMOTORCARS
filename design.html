00

What this sheet is for

A foundry specimen, not documentation. One measured column: key on the left, the real component in the middle, the reading on the right. Nothing is boxed — separation is rule and air, which is the same instruction the pages themselves follow.

Every sample renders from the live tokens and the live component CSS, and every contrast pair is measured at runtime against the threshold its own rendered size requires. Chapter 01 lists what that measurement caught — this sheet exists to find defects, not to present a finished system.

01

What the sheet caught

Five rules were being broken by work already shipped to the branch. Each was found by reading the rule, not by looking at the screen — which is the point: none of them looked wrong.

spacing D4

was — mobile gutter clamped to 20px

now — 24px floor. Below it a phone view earns “generic app”.

spacing D2

was — sections padded symmetrically

now — bottom-heavy 1:1.5. Reading runs downward, so equal padding reads as bottom-tight and shoves each section into the next.

spacing I1

was — one uniform gap in every text stack

now — a .stack with differentiated relationships. A uniform gap flattens a heading and its lead into a list.

motion I3

was — one easing curve for everything

now — ease-out enters, ease-in exits. The inversion is named as the commonest reason interface motion reads as sluggish.

motion D2

was — scale(1.02) on the card photograph

now — a firming rule. Scale is banned outright: it resamples the frame, and the hover budget is one move.

03b

Why there is no serif

Nine pairings were proposed and rejected before the axis turned out to be wrong rather than the names. The answer came from Alex's own approved vault, five entries deep, all in:

augen-pro

“Типографика почти полностью нейтральная. Характер создаётся не выбором «интересного шрифта», а масштабом, прозрачностью, положением и движением”

ruadh-com

“Никакого стандартного luxury serif. Один сдержанный grotesk — характер создают фотографии, crop и композиция”

beings-co · 3

“выразительные портреты, oversized grotesk, два контрастных цвета, большие поля”

immersive-g · 3

“очень крупная типографика”

This is a named yield on the dialect's three-voice rule, which asks for a didone display. The evidence for setting it aside is Alex's own judgements rather than preference. It is not “never a serif” — ciridae and kinncollective are serif-led and also approved. The discriminator is that the face is not asked to carry the character.

Rejected, in order: Bodoni Moda + Archivo · Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans · Bodoni corrected to 400 / opsz 96 / +0.005 tracking · Cormorant Garamond, whose old-style figures break tabular alignment · Fraunces · Space Grotesk · Anton · mono at display · Archivo 800 wide + 400. The first Bodoni failed on my settings, not the family: 600 and −0.035 thickens a didone's hairlines and makes it ordinary.

06

The card, and its loading state

The photograph is the card and the whole card is the target — no border, no fill, no button. The skeleton matches the card's real dimensions, so the swap shifts nothing; it pulses opacity and never sweeps a shimmer, which is the template look.
