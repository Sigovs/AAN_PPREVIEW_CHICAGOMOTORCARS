Design system

Chicago Motor Cars · internal reference

Every specimen below renders with the real tokens and the real component CSS. The page measures its own contrast at runtime — each ratio is computed from the element's own colour composited over its own ground, at its own rendered size, against the AA threshold that size actually requires. If a token moves and breaks a pair, the number here turns red. Nothing on this page is typed in by hand.

Ground

Near-black, never #000: pure black flattens photography and leaves no darker step to put underneath a light pool. One light band exists, used once, for the record section.

Ink on the dark ground

Warm off-white, never #fff. Three levels only. Anything a visitor must read sits at --ink-3 or above.

--ink Sold to a collector in Monterey
--ink-2 Supporting copy, plate values, roster rows
--ink-3 Micro-labels · the 14px floor

--ink-3 was 0.42 and measured 3.7:1 — below AA. Every mono micro-label is functional text, so it carries the same floor as body copy: the alpha came up, the size did not come down.

Accent

The existing CMC red, measured off the current site and carried through unchanged under the REDESIGN mandate. The dialect prefers a desaturated accent; that rule is set aside here on its stated condition — the brand is colour-led. The fix is rationing, not re-hueing.

--accent
--accent-deep · hover
on --accent White label on the fill — the only text allowed on red

Roles, decided up front and not extended: one primary fill, and one hover step. Red never carries small text on the ground — that pair measures 3.4:1, which is a fill-and-rule number, not a text number. Budget on a screen: under 5% of pixels.

The vehicle card

The photograph is the card and the whole card is the target. No border, no fill, and no button — a filled CTA repeated down a grid turns a decision point into wallpaper, and it is the single loudest defect on the current site, where “More Details” appears 299 times.

West Chicago 2019 McLaren Senna Exposed blue tinted carbon fibre · CPO warranty $3,499,8002,615 miCARFAX Naperville 2021 Koenigsegg Regera Ghost package · one of eighty $2,899,8002,055 miCARFAX Rock Hill 2024 Rimac Nevera 1,914 hp · full electric $1,999,8003,498 miCARFAX

Images are AI placeholders. The card is deliberately built to survive an ordinary photograph: the frame is a fixed 4:3, the ground behind it is a raised step rather than a hole, and no type sits on the image except the showroom pill, which carries its own scrim.

Limit of this page: the runtime measurement walks up to the nearest painted background, so it cannot see image pixels. Every text-over-photograph pair — the showroom pill here, the hero copy on index — is verified by sampling the composited render, not by this page. A self-measuring page that silently cannot measure the hardest case is worse than one that says so.

Motion

Two durations, one easing. Motion explains a state change and gets out of the way; nothing loops, nothing moves on entry, and the static build stands alone. Everything is neutralised under prefers-reduced-motion.

--dur-1 · 140ms · colour, opacity --dur-2 · 240ms · transform, borders --ease · cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
