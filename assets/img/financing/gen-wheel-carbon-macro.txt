SUBJECT
  Extreme macro: a forged alloy wheel, the fender arch above it, and a
  band of exposed twill carbon fibre along the arch. Camera at hub
  height, oblique three-quarter. Single hard key light raking the rim
  lip and the weave; the rest falls to near-black.

  Only a fragment of a car is in frame. It is not readable as any
  particular vehicle, which is the point — the brief asked for
  automotive sculpture rather than a car photograph.

  · No premises. The ground is a black studio floor with no architecture.

  VERDICT: passes. This is the safest class of synthetic automotive
  subject precisely because it claims nothing about a specific car.

WHY IT EXISTS — the composition problem it solves
  Read as one object, the homepage shows the car eight times and always
  from a distance: whole, in an environment, as an object of desire. It
  never once gets close. There is no material anywhere on the page — no
  surface, no carbon, no machined metal.

  Financing is the ownership transition, so it is the right and only
  place for the page's single moment of physical contact with a car.
  The image is the chapter's mass; the controls sit in its own dark
  zones rather than in a panel beside it.
