assets/img/stories/ — the story hero frames
===========================================

ORIGIN. Ten hero frames — nine studio frames of Chicago Motor Cars' own inventory, taken
2026-08-30 from the hero image of each post on their blog index:

    https://www.chicagomotorcars.com/blog/

Source files were served from chicagomotorcars.com/wp-content/uploads/,
and their filenames are the vehicles' own listing titles — for example
"Used-2018-Porsche-911-GT2-RS-Weissach-Magnesium-Wheels-Paint-to-Sample-
Volcano-Grey-Full-PPF-1755890895.jpg".

NOT SYNTHETIC. These are CMC's own listing photographs of cars they
had for sale — the white-seamless turntable set used throughout their
inventory. No `gen-` prefix is owed and none is used.

WHAT WAS DONE. Cropping and resizing only:

    magick <src> -auto-orient -strip -resize 1400x788^ \
                 -gravity center -extent 1400x788 -quality 80

    ...and the same at 700x394 for the grid cards.

No retouching, no colour grading. 4.3 MB of originals became 1.8 MB.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE OF THE TEN IS A STOCK PHOTOGRAPH, AND IT IS THEIRS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

"Luxury Vehicle Inspections in Chicago" carries, on CMC own site,
Chicago-Motor-Cars-Service-Top-1.jpg (1410x600) — a stock frame of a
smiling mechanic in a yellow-accented uniform. It is not a CMC
technician and not a CMC workshop.

An earlier pass swapped it for assets/img/warranty/gen-brake-wheel.
Alex direction is to take what is on chicagomotorcars.com, so their
frame is used, unaltered, exactly as they publish it. This is a mirror
of a picture already live on their site, not a new claim made here.

It is still worth asking CMC for a frame of their own service bay: it
is the weakest image on the page and the only one that is not of their
own property.
