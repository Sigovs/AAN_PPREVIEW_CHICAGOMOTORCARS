assets/img/instagram/ — provenance sidecar
==========================================

WHAT THESE ARE
  The twelve frames Chicago Motor Cars' own homepage is serving in its
  Instagram feed, taken from the source Alex named on 2026-08-14:
  https://www.chicagomotorcars.com/

  The feed is rendered client-side by Smash Balloon (Instagram Feed Pro),
  so the URLs are not in the served HTML. They were read from the live
  DOM — the .sbi_photo elements carry the image as a background-image —
  and fetched from CMC's own wp-content, not from Instagram's CDN:

    https://www.chicagomotorcars.com/wp-content/uploads/
      sb-instagram-feed-images/<id>full.jpg

  Downloaded 2026-08-14. The post each frame belongs to is on
  instagram.com/chicagomotorcars; the feed order on that homepage is the
  order below, and the captions there are what the filenames are from.

NOT SYNTHETIC
  Every frame is CMC's own photography of CMC's own inventory, shot on
  their turntable against their wordmark backdrop. Nothing here is
  generated, so no gen- prefix and no generated-imagery audit applies.
  GI3 is satisfied by the plainest route there is: these are real
  photographs of the real cars, taken by the dealer selling them.

  Eleven are 3:2. The Countach frame is a 9:16 reel and is the reason the
  contact sheet is built on a common HEIGHT rather than a common width:
  at one height its native aspect makes it the narrow frame in the row,
  which is what a real feed looks like. Cropping it to 3:2 would have
  thrown away most of the picture to make it match.

SIZE
  ~45-50KB each, ~140KB for the reel; 640px is what CMC's own site
  serves and the sheet renders them at 280px tall, so every frame is
  downscaled and none is upscaled. No responsive ladder: a 640px master
  is already below the smallest step a ladder would add.

THESE REPLACE
  assets/img/stock/ in the Instagram section only. Those ten frames are
  ~326px wide, scraped from CMC inventory listings, and the section was
  upscaling them by up to 1.54x. They stay where they are; nothing else
  referenced them from this chapter.

OPEN
