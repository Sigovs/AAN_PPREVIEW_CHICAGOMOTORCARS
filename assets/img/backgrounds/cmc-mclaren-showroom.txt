SUBJECT
  A dark showroom: McLaren 675LT Spider (dark), Senna (silver, lit) and
  P1 (blue) on a polished reflecting floor, against a wall carrying a
  large printed mural of McLaren race and road cars. No people in the
  room; the mural includes printed portraits of drivers, which are
  artwork on a wall rather than photographs of people on the premises.

WHY IT SUITS THIS SECTION
  Its left third is near-black and its lower third is empty reflected
  floor — exactly where this chapter puts its copy and its figures. The
  scrims that carried them over the Ford GT frame have come down by half
  because this picture does not need them.

OPEN QUESTIONS — resolve before this goes to the client
  1. ORIGIN NOT DECLARED, and this one matters more than most. The frame
     reads as a McLaren-produced showroom or press image: the lighting is
     staged, the floor reflection is even, and the three cars are a
     factory line-up rather than a dealer's floor.

     It is NOT confirmed as CMC's premises and NOT confirmed as CMC's
     cars. The copy beside it says "Built on more than exceptional cars"
     and gives CMC's own figures, so on this page the room reads as CMC's
     room. If it is McLaren's, that is a claim the picture is making for
     the business that the business has not made.

     Same call as sell/ and warranty/: NO gen- prefix, because asserting
     synthesis is as wrong as asserting photography.

  2. THE MURAL. It carries McLaren marques and driver portraits. If the
     frame is McLaren's own asset the mural comes with it; if it is a
     third party's photograph of a McLaren showroom, both the photograph
     and the artwork in it are someone else's.

  Ask Alex where it came from. If it is not CMC's own room, the honest
  fixes are a CMC showroom photograph or a frame with no other brand's
  identity printed across the back wall.
