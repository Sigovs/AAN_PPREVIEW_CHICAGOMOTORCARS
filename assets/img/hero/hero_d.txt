OPEN — the one thing this file cannot state
-------------------------------------------
Whether this frame is CMC's own photography or a press/stock image is
NOT established. It matters, and not on a technicality:

  * the art direction says "real inventory cars rather than universal
    stock supercars" — a stock Senna is exactly the thing it rules out;
  * the ten frames in assets/img/stock/ are unmistakably CMC's, wordmark
    burned into the top band. This one carries no such mark, and it was
    shot somewhere that is not a Chicago showroom;
  * a dealership hero implies the car is on the floor. If it is not, the
    page makes a claim the business cannot stand behind.

Needed before this goes anywhere near a client: confirmation that CMC
owns or is licensed for this frame, and ideally that the car was theirs.
