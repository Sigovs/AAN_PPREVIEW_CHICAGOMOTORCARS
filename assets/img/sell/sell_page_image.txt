OPEN QUESTIONS — resolve before this goes to the client
  1. ORIGIN NOT DECLARED. The frame reads as a 3D render rather than a
     photograph: the reflections, the light falloff and the showroom
     geometry are all cleaner than a shot interior. It has NOT been
     confirmed as photographic, and it has NOT been confirmed as CMC's
     own premises or CMC's own cars.

     This matters because of the project's GI3 rule: nothing synthetic
     may depict a real CMC car, a real CMC showroom or a real person. On
     a dealership page a showroom interior reads as THIS dealership's
     showroom. If the frame is synthetic, GI3 applies and the file needs
     the gen- prefix and a full generated-imagery audit; if it is a real
     photograph of somewhere that is not CMC, the art direction's "real
     inventory cars, not stock supercars" term applies instead.

     Until Alex confirms which, the file carries NO gen- prefix, because
     asserting synthesis is as wrong as asserting photography.

  2. THIRD-PARTY SIGNATURE. There is a handwritten signature in the
     bottom right of the frame, at roughly x95% / y82% — an artist's or
     photographer's mark. It survives into the composited page at wide
     viewports: the bone ramp at the foot of the band knocks it back but
     does not cover it. Below about 1500px the band crops narrower than
     the frame and the mark is cropped out with the right edge.

     It has been left in place. Removing someone's signature from their
     work is a rights question, not a layout one, and is Alex's call —
     either the licence covers it, or the frame should be replaced.
