assets/img/team/ — the CMC staff portraits
==========================================

ORIGIN. All thirteen portraits are Chicago Motor Cars' OWN photographs of
their OWN staff, downloaded 2026-08-30 from their live team page:

    https://www.chicagomotorcars.com/team-chicago-motor-cars-in-chicago-il/

Source files were served from chicagomotorcars.com/wp-content/uploads/.

NOT SYNTHETIC. Nothing in this folder is generated, and nothing in it may
be. `generated-imagery` GI3 forbids a synthetic depiction of a real
person outright, and a dealership's staff page is the clearest case of
that rule there is. No `gen-` prefix is owed and none is used. If a
portrait is ever missing, the answer is to ask CMC for the frame — never
to make one.

WHAT WAS DONE TO THEM. Cropping and resizing only:

    magick <src> -auto-orient -strip -resize 760x950^ \
                 -gravity north -extent 760x950 -quality 82

    ...and the same at 380x475 for the roster plates.

`-gravity north` keeps the head where the photographer put it; every one
of the thirteen was checked on a contact sheet afterwards and no face is
clipped. 6.0 MB of originals became 1.5 MB across 26 files.

NO RETOUCHING, AND THE MONOCHROME IS NOT BAKED IN. The portraits are
desaturated by CSS in team.css, not in these files. Two reasons: the
files stay as CMC shot them, and the treatment can be reversed or
revisited without re-processing anything. What sits here is colour.

WHY THE TREATMENT EXISTS. The set comes from at least three shoots — a
lit showroom series with the purple and blue Lamborghinis behind the
subject, a white-seamless studio series, and phone frames under office
fluorescents. Side by side in colour they do not read as one page.

THE SIX WHO ARE NOT HERE. Parin Shah, Travis Papenberg, Ali Mafee, Frank
Sacco, Gary Blanchard and James Sealey have no photograph on CMC's own
page — they carry a shared grey "Staff-coming.jpg" placeholder there.
That placeholder is deliberately NOT copied into this project; the page
gives them an authored initials plate instead. Ask CMC for these six
frames before the page goes live.
