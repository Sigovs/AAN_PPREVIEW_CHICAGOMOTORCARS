THIS ONE IS SYNTHETIC, AND THE OTHER FOUR ARE NOT
  It carries the gen- prefix and they do not. The prefix travels with
  the file precisely so this distinction survives the file being moved,
  renamed in a CMS, or handed to someone who never read this note.

  It does not need a pixel-level judgement to establish. NEWPORT BEACH
  HAS NOT OPENED — the panel's own caption says "California — opening
  soon" — so no photograph of its showroom can exist. Whatever this
  frame is, it is not a picture of the thing it sits under. The visual

  ground-lighting placed rather than grown.

GI3 AUDIT — THIS IS THE RULE IT ENGAGES
  The project's standing rule: nothing synthetic depicts a real CMC car,
  a real CMC showroom or a real person.

  The frame depicts BOTH a showroom and a car, and the placement makes
  each reading stronger, not weaker:

  1. THE SHOWROOM. It sits in position five of a row of four verified
     aerials of real premises. The row is the claim. A synthetic
     building in the fifth slot inherits the credibility of the four
     beside it, and a reader has no reason to treat one card in a
     uniform row differently from its neighbours.

  2. THE CAR. A silver 911 stands at the entrance. On a dealership page
     a car in front of a showroom reads as stock. This one is rendered,
     so it is a picture of a car that does not exist, positioned where
     inventory would be.

  WHAT MAKES IT DEFENSIBLE, AND WHAT DOES NOT
  A rendering of a PLANNED showroom is a legitimate and ordinary thing
  for a business to publish — architectural visualisation is a named
  category and nobody is deceived by it. What makes it a problem here is
  not the image, it is that the page does not say what the image is. The
  caption states the location is opening soon; it does not state that
  the picture is a rendering, and those are different claims.

  THE FIX IS ONE WORD ON THE PANEL. A label — "Rendering", or "Concept"
  — in the caption's existing micro register, on this panel only. It
  costs nothing, it resolves GI3 completely, and it turns the odd panel
  from a thing that might be caught into a thing that was declared.
