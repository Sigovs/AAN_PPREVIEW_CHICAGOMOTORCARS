THE POSTER IS A REAL FRAME OF THE FILM, at t=8s, captured off the
decoded video rather than drawn or sourced elsewhere. That matters:
if the video never loads — a slow connection, a blocked script,
prefers-reduced-motion — the band is still a photograph of the same
room at the same moment, not a black rectangle or a different place.

SUBJECT of the film: a slow walk through a large collection space.
Rows of Mustang Cobra R in white and red, a Ford GT, later two
Aventadors (a blue SVJ and a light blue SV Roadster) against a wall
carrying Lamborghini, Ferrari and GM murals. Polished concrete floor
with strong reflections. No people, no plates readable.

NOT SYNTHETIC. It is a filmed walkthrough, so generated-imagery does
not apply to it and no gen- prefix is owed.

UNCONFIRMED, AND IT MATTERS BEFORE THIS GOES TO A CLIENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ORIGIN. Whether this is CMC's own showroom, another dealer's, a
   private collection or a museum is not established. The murals read
   more like a collection space than a sales floor. The same open
   question sits on assets/img/hero/hero_d.txt and on
   assets/img/backgrounds/README.txt.

2. WHAT IT IMPLIES. It runs directly under the inventory section, so
   it reads as CMC's own room and CMC's own cars. If it is neither,
   the page is making a claim the film cannot support, and the art
   direction's "real inventory cars, not stock supercars" is the rule
   it breaks.

3. WEIGHT. 20.2 MB for a 27-second band. It is lazy-loaded and never
   fetched under reduced motion, so it costs nothing until a visitor
   reaches it — but for anyone who does reach it on a phone it is a
   real 20 MB. An H.264 pass at a sane bitrate, or a shorter cut,
   would fix it. No ffmpeg on this machine.
