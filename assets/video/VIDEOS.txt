vid-cmc-vault.mp4 / vid-naperville-karma.mp4 — the Our Videos films
====================================================================

ORIGIN. Both are Chicago Motor Cars' OWN films of their OWN showrooms,
downloaded 2026-08-30 from their live Our Videos page:

    https://www.chicagomotorcars.com/our-videos-chicago-il/

Their source URLs, verbatim:

    .../assets/images/1CMC%20Basement%20Website%20Video.mp4
    .../assets/images/Karma%20Naperville%20Video.mov

NOT SYNTHETIC. Filmed footage, so `generated-imagery` does not apply and
no gen- prefix is owed. The same standing as assets/video/hero-video.mp4,
which Alex confirmed as client footage on 2026-08-10.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS WRONG WITH THE ORIGINALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

    file                        codec   size        result
    1CMC Basement Website .mp4  h264    3840x2160   51.8 MB   87.2s
    Karma Naperville .mov       HEVC    1920x1080   78.3 MB   76.6s

130 MB on one page, and the Naperville film is HEVC in a QuickTime
container — a combination Chrome and Firefox do not decode. On CMC's
live site most visitors get a black rectangle where that film should
be. This is a real defect on their page, not a stylistic objection, and
it is worth telling them about regardless of what happens to this
redesign.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

    ffmpeg -i <src> -vf scale=1280:-2 -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 24 \
           -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart \
           -c:a aac -b:a 96k <out>

    vid-cmc-vault.mp4         1280x720   15.5 MB   (was 51.8)
    vid-naperville-karma.mp4  1280x720   13.9 MB   (was 78.3)

29 MB together, down from 130, and both now play everywhere. No frames
were cut, no colour was graded, nothing was retouched — this is a
re-encode, not an edit.

NOTE ON THE TOOLING. assets/video/README.txt records "No ffmpeg on this
machine, so it has not been done" against the 20 MB break band. That is
no longer true: ffmpeg 8.1.2 is installed. The break band is still
un-recompressed because nobody has asked, but the blocker named in that
file is gone.
