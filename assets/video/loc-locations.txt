NOT SYNTHETIC — AND THAT IS THE POINT
  These two panels are the first location media on the page that is not
  a placeholder. The other three still carry stand-in frames pulled from
  the Instagram set, and none of those was taken at the location it sits
  under. That gap is loudest now, not quieter: two panels playing their
  own building make the three that do not look like the ones with
  something to hide. Worth closing before this goes to the client.

  Nothing here is generated, so no gen- prefix and no generated-imagery
  audit applies. GI3 is satisfied directly.

HOW THEY ARE USED
  Background of their own accordion panel, under the same grade as the
  stills and under the panel's existing scrim, which sits ABOVE the film
  so the caption keeps its ground when the video fades up.

  preload="none" and the source's URL held in data-src, so a visitor who
  never opens the panel never downloads it. Attached on first open,
  played while open or hovered, paused the moment it closes. Never
  started under prefers-reduced-motion — the still underneath is the
  whole picture there and it is already in place.
