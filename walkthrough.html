The page, top to bottom

Fourteen chapters, one scroll

All business content on the page is real: your inventory, addresses, phone numbers, Instagram feed and published reviews. Nothing is placeholder copy. The two chapters marked with a red rule are where we'd like a decision from you.

01

Hero

Two slides. The 01 / 02 control at the left switches between them — a second car and a second claim, without pushing the visitor anywhere. The search panel sits on the right and stays put while the picture changes.

02

Featured Inventory

We cut the cars out of their backgrounds instead of showing them parked on the showroom floor. It takes more work per vehicle and it reads considerably more expensive — the car becomes the object, not the room it happens to be standing in. We can show you the version with the backgrounds left in if you'd like to compare.

03

"Some cars only make sense in person"

A full-width film band that names the four showrooms and sends the visitor to plan a visit.

04

Sell your car

Get an offer, with the three-step sequence beneath it — tell us about the car, receive an offer, payment and pickup.

05

Built on more than exceptional cars

Your three figures at scale: 30,000+ vehicles sold, since 2003, $2B+ in worldwide sales. 30,000+ leads and the other two support it, because one number people remember beats three they don't.

06

Warranty & Protection

The claim over a full-width frame, then the three provisions — every part covered, multiple plans, aftermarket protection.

07

Financing

Three ways to start — online credit approval, payment calculator, trade valuation. BitPay is present and deliberately quiet: it's a real capability, but on the homepage it shouldn't stand level with getting approved.

08

Our Services

The six-item service register over your workshop footage.

09

About us

Your own words, taken from your About page.

10

Karma — authorized dealer

A brand inside the brand: the Karma mark, one line, one route, over Karma's own headlight footage — dimmed hard, so the chapter stays yours rather than becoming their advertisement.

11

Instagram

Your twelve most recent posts, pulled from the feed your current site already serves. The strip scrolls and can be dragged.

12

Reviews

Nine real reviews across Google, Cars.com and DealerRater, taken from your own testimonials page. Names, dates and wording exactly as published, three per page, each card linking back to its source.

13

Come see what's on the floor

All four showrooms — West Chicago, Naperville, Rock Hill and Tonganoxie — with addresses and tap-to-call numbers, plus Newport Beach standing as the fifth. That one carries no address or phone by design: it shows where and when, and nothing we'd have had to make up.

14

Footer

Wordmark, three columns of links, your four social accounts, legal line.

Two calls we'd like your opinion on

Both are reversible — say the word

Cut-out cars in Featured Inventory

Removing the dealership background puts the car on the page's own ground with nothing competing for attention. It is the single biggest reason that section reads premium rather than like a listing site. The trade is that it takes preparation on every new vehicle, and it hides the showroom.

If you'd like to see it the other way, we'll build the same section with the photographs untouched and put the two side by side.

Currently: cut out

Photography, and what we did to get here

There isn't a usable image library yet, so several frames have been stretched, re-cropped and repositioned to fit their sections, and in a few places cars have been composited together to make a frame that works.

It holds up at preview size. It will not hold up on a large screen, and it is the one thing standing between this page and finished.
